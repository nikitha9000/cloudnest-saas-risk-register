CloudNest SaaS – Cybersecurity Risk Register
Project Overview

A cybersecurity risk assessment project for a fictional 10-employee SaaS startup, CloudNest.

This project demonstrates how organizations identify, assess, prioritize, and mitigate cybersecurity and operational risks.
Objectives

    Identify cybersecurity and operational risks.

    Assess likelihood and business impact.

    Calculate inherent risk scores.

    Prioritize risks using risk ratings.

    Develop mitigation strategies.

    Assign risk owners.

    Assess residual risks.

    Establish periodic risk reviews.

Risk Assessment Methodology

Risk Score = Likelihood × Impact
Score	Risk Rating
1–5	Low
6–14	Medium
15–25	High
Risks Identified

    Cloud storage misconfiguration

    Credential compromise

    Application vulnerabilities

    Phishing attacks

    Ransomware

    Cloud service outages

    Accidental data deletion

    Former employee access

    Third-party vendor compromise

    Delayed incident detection
